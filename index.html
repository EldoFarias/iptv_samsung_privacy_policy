Privacy Policy — ODLE IPTV

This Privacy Policy applies to all versions of the ODLE IPTV application, available on the following platforms:

📺 Samsung Smart TV (Tizen) 📺 Roku 📱 Android / Android TV 📺 LG Smart TV (webOS) 🖥️ Windows

1. Introduction

ODLE IPTV ("the App") is a media player application developed by ODLE Streaming that allows users to watch IPTV content from playlists they configure themselves. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using the App on any supported platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Device Identifier

To generate a unique license key and verify your subscription, the App reads a hardware identifier from your device. The method varies by platform:

PlatformIdentifier Used
Samsung Smart TV (Tizen)Device Unique ID (DUID) provided by the Tizen firmware
RokuDevice Unique Service ID provided by the Roku OS
Android / Android TVAndroid ID or hardware fingerprint
LG Smart TV (webOS)Device serial or MAC address provided by webOS
WindowsHardware fingerprint (CPU, screen, browser agent)

These identifiers do not change across reinstalls and are used solely to bind your license to your device. A logical MAC address derived from the same identifier is also used as a secondary license reference.

2.2 Subscription and Activation Data

When you activate a license or subscription, the following data is sent to our servers:

  • Device identifier (as described above)
  • Logical MAC address
  • App version
  • Activation code (if provided by a reseller)
  • Email address (only if you choose to pay via PIX and voluntarily provide it for a receipt)

We do not collect your name or payment card information directly. Payments are processed by third-party services (e.g., Mercado Pago) and are subject to their own privacy policies.

2.3 Playlist and Content Data

Playlist URLs and credentials are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • M3U playlist URLs and Xtream Codes credentials (host, username, password) entered by the user are stored locally on the device using the platform's native storage (e.g., local file cache, app registry, or localStorage).
  • We do not transmit your playlist URLs, credentials, channel lists, or viewing history to our servers.
  • Content metadata (channel names, movie posters, EPG data) is fetched from URLs you provide and cached locally on your device.

2.4 Usage Data

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry. The App does not include any third-party analytics SDK on any platform.

All collected data is used exclusively for license management. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties except as required by law.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Device identifiers are stored on our servers only for the duration of your active license.
  • Playlist URLs and credentials are stored locally on your device and never transmitted to us.
  • All communication between the App and our license servers uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • Upon license expiration or deletion request, your device record is removed from our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may interact with the following third-party services:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database): used to fetch movie and series metadata (titles, posters, descriptions). See TMDB Privacy Policy.
  • Mercado Pago: used for PIX payment processing. See Mercado Pago Privacy Policy.
  • User-provided IPTV servers: the App loads streams and metadata from URLs provided by the user. These servers are not operated by ODLE Streaming and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your device's license record from our servers at any time by contacting us at eldofarias81@outlook.com. Upon deletion, your license will be deactivated and all associated data removed from our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new platforms or changes in our practices. We will notify users by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
