Privacy Policy — ODLE IPTV

Last updated: June 4, 2026  |  Developer: ODLE Streaming  |  Contact: eldofarias81@outlook.com

1. Introduction

ODLE IPTV ("the App") is a media streaming application developed by ODLE Streaming for Samsung Smart TV (Tizen platform). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Device Information

To generate a unique license identifier and verify your subscription, the App reads:

  • Device Unique ID (DUID): a hardware identifier provided by Samsung Tizen firmware. This value does not change across reinstalls and is used solely to bind your license to your device.
  • Network MAC address: used as a secondary device identifier for license verification.

2.2 Subscription and Activation Data

When you activate a license or subscription, the following data is sent to our servers:

  • Device ID (DUID)
  • App version
  • Activation code (if provided by a reseller)

We do not collect your name, email address, or payment information directly. Payments are processed by third-party services and are subject to their own privacy policies.

2.3 Playlist and Content Data

  • M3U playlist URLs and Xtream Codes credentials entered by the user are stored locally on the device only (Samsung Tizen localStorage).
  • We do not transmit your playlist URLs, credentials, or viewing history to our servers.
  • Content metadata (channel names, movie posters) is fetched from the URLs you provide and cached locally on your device.

2.4 Usage Data

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry. The App does not include any third-party analytics SDK.

All collected data is used exclusively for license management. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties except as required by law.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Device identifiers are stored on our servers only for the duration of your active license.
  • Playlist URLs and credentials are stored locally on your device and never transmitted to us.
  • Communication between the App and our license servers uses HTTPS.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may load content (images, streams) from URLs provided by the user. These URLs point to third-party IPTV servers that are not operated by ODLE Streaming. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these servers.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your device's license record from our servers by contacting us at eldofarias81@outlook.com. Upon deletion, your license will be deactivated.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
