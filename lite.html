This Privacy Policy applies to ODLE IPTV Lite (package com.farias.odlestreaming.lite), a lightweight version of ODLE IPTV for Android phones, Android TV and TV boxes, distributed on Google Play and as a direct download.

1. Introduction

ODLE IPTV Lite ("the App") is a media player that plays content from playlists the user configures. The App does not provide, host or sell any channels, movies or series. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Device Identifier

To bind your license to your device, the App generates a logical MAC address and a device key (format ODLE-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Both are derived from a one-way SHA-256 hash of device build information (build ID, manufacturer, brand, model, device and hardware names). They are not your network card's real MAC address, cannot be reversed into the original information, and stay the same across reinstalls on the same device. It is the same identifier used by ODLE IPTV, so a license activated on a device is valid in both apps.

2.2 License, Activation and Payment Data

The following data is sent to our servers to check, activate or purchase a license:

  • Logical MAC address and device key
  • App version
  • Activation code (only if you enter one provided by a reseller)
  • Device manufacturer, model and build ID (only when activating with a reseller code)
  • Email address (only when you pay via PIX — it is required by Mercado Pago to issue the payment)
  • For purchases made through Google Play: product ID, purchase token and order ID, plus an anonymous account identifier (a SHA-256 hash of the logical MAC address) used to credit the license to the correct device

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts or payment card information. Payments are processed by Mercado Pago (PIX) or Google Play, which are subject to their own privacy policies.

2.3 Playlist and Content Data

Playlists you add, favorites and watch progress are stored locally on your device only.
  • M3U playlist URLs and Xtream Codes credentials (server, username, password) that you enter are stored locally on the device and are never sent to our servers.
  • If your reseller links a playlist to your license, its details are delivered to your device by our server, as part of the license check.
  • Channel lists, posters, synopses and program guide (EPG) are loaded directly from the IPTV server of your playlist and cached locally on your device.
  • Favorites, watch progress and settings are stored locally and are not sent to our servers.

2.4 Usage Data

We do not collect analytics, crash reports or usage telemetry. The App does not include any third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • License records are stored on our servers only while needed to manage your license.
  • Playlist URLs, credentials, favorites and watch progress stay on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • Communication with our license servers uses HTTPS.
  • Upon a deletion request, your device record is removed from our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: used for purchases when the App is installed from Google Play. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Mercado Pago: used for PIX payment processing. See Mercado Pago Privacy Policy.
  • User-provided IPTV servers: the App loads streams, images and program guides from the servers in the playlists you add. These servers are not operated by ODLE Streaming and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load playlists and streams and to check the license.
  • Google Play Billing: to purchase a license through Google Play.

The App does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your device's license record from our servers at any time by contacting us at eldofarias81@outlook.com. Upon deletion, your license will be deactivated and all associated data removed from our servers. Data stored on your device can be removed at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
